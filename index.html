An arsenal that evolves

  • Elite minionsSummon defenders to stand between you and the mob.

Under the hood

About 30,000 lines of C#. A few of the systems behind it.

Before
After

Cut the game's Android size by streaming videos, cutscenes and music from a Cloudflare CDN via Addressables, subsetting CJK fonts and capping textures.

3-tier fallback

  1. Remote Config
  2. Local cache
  3. Embedded defaults

Live-tunable balance on UGS Remote Config, so weapons, spells, enemies and difficulty can be tuned without a client update.

16 IAPs, 173 regions

The full free-to-play stack: Unity Purchasing, LevelPlay rewarded and interstitial ads, a Tapjoy offerwall and Google UMP consent, with regional prices from a World Bank PPP model.

Hidden difficulty

An adaptive system: a cloud-synced per-player rating, fed by defence HP, headshot ratio and deaths, scales only stats with no on-screen readout, with a Remote Config kill switch.

Cloud save

Unity Authentication and Cloud Save, linked to Google Play Games, Apple Game Center and Steam, with conflict-safe progress merging for offline play.

Mobile and Steam, one codebase

The Steam version lives on the same branch as mobile, with one policy layer for platform differences and PC saves keyed by Steam ID, so they load offline and stay separate per account.

12 languages

Unity Localization, including Chinese, Japanese and Korean, with per-language fonts and automated glyph and text-overflow checks.
